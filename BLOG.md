# Simplify Shipment Tracking with Our New AfterShip Node for n8n

We're excited to announce the release of our latest community node for n8n: the AfterShip integration! At Velocity BPA, we're constantly building automation solutions that solve real business challenges, and tracking shipments across multiple carriers is one we hear about frequently.

## The Shipment Tracking Challenge

If you're running an e-commerce business, managing logistics, or handling customer support, you know the pain of shipment tracking. Customers want updates. Support teams need visibility. And when you're shipping through multiple carriers—FedEx today, DHL tomorrow, a regional carrier next week—keeping track becomes a nightmare.

Manually checking carrier websites doesn't scale. Building custom integrations for 1100+ carriers? That's not realistic.

## Enter the AfterShip Node for n8n

Our new AfterShip node brings powerful shipment tracking automation directly into your n8n workflows. AfterShip consolidates tracking across 1100+ carriers worldwide, and now you can leverage that power within your existing automation flows.

## Key Features

With this node, you can:

- **Track shipments** across 1100+ carriers from a single integration
- **Generate shipping labels** programmatically
- **Manage returns** and streamline your reverse logistics
- **Process orders** and sync shipment data with your other systems
- **Automate customer notifications** based on shipment status changes
- **Connect tracking data** to your CRM, helpdesk, or analytics platforms

Imagine triggering a personalized email when a package is out for delivery, automatically creating support tickets for delayed shipments, or updating your inventory system when returns are initiated—all without writing complex code.

## Getting Started

Installing the AfterShip node is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-aftership


After restarting n8n, you'll find the AfterShip node ready to use in your workflow editor.

For detailed documentation and to contribute, visit our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Velocity-BPA/n8n-nodes-aftership
